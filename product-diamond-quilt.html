What you get

Soft-touch Nappa-grade PU quilted in a 40 mm diamond, cut on your car's own seat template and fitted at the studio in 3–4 hours.

  • Front pair, rear bench and split-backrest covers cut to your model
  • Five headrest covers with matching quilt
  • Armrest and rear-centre console covers where your car has them
  • Studio fitting by two fitters, seats out where needed
  • Care kit: pH-neutral cleaner and microfibre cloth

Caring for it

A few minutes a month keeps it looking new for years. The warranty covers stitching and fitting; see Warranty & care.

  • Wipe weekly with a damp microfibre cloth
  • Use a pH-neutral cleaner for spills; never solvents or polish
  • Park in shade or use a sunshade in summer to keep the PU supple
  • Re-tighten service free at 30 days if anything loosens
