7D, rubber or carpet? A buyer's guide to car floor mats

Floor mats take more abuse than any other part of the cabin. We compare the three main types and explain why fit matters more than thickness.

Deepa SubramanianAccessories specialist · 30 Aug 2026 · 6 min read

A good mat protects the carpet, traps dirt and stays put under the pedals. A bad one slides forward, bunches up and can even catch the brake pedal. Fit matters more than anything else.

The three main types

TypeBest atWatch out forFrom
7D / 5D mouldedFull coverage up the sidesTakes up a little foot space₹5,900
Rubber traysMud and monsoon waterCan look plain, warms in sun₹2,400
Tufted carpetComfort and quietHold water, slow to dry₹3,200

Why 7D mats are so popular

7D mats are made from layers of leatherette, foam and an anti-skid base, moulded to the exact shape of the footwell. They cover the side walls where shoes scuff, and a removable top layer of grass or carpet catches sand. They are easy to wipe and look tidy.

Fit and safety

  • Choose mats made for your exact model and year, not 'universal'.
  • Make sure the driver's mat clips onto the factory anchor hooks.
  • Never stack a new mat on top of the old one.
  • Check the pedals travel fully with the mat in place.

Our pick for most owners

A 7D mat set for daily use with a rubber tray kept in the boot for the monsoon. Swap the tray in when the rain starts and the carpet underneath stays dry all season.

Studio tip

Clean 7D mats with a damp cloth and a mild soap. Lift the top grass layer and shake it out weekly.
