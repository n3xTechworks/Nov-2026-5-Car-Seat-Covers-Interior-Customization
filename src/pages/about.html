What we believe

Templates, not sizes.

Every model gets its own paper pattern. That is the whole difference between a cover and upholstery.

Show before we cut.

You see materials on your own seats and approve a sample board before any leather is cut.

Fix it free.

Two years on every stitch, plus a free re-tightening visit at 30 days.

Since 2014

How we got here

  1. 2014

    First stall

    Two tailors, one machine, hatchback covers only, in a rented shed off Gandhipuram.

  2. 2017

    Pattern library

    Started keeping paper templates for every model we fit, so the second Swift took half the time.

  3. 2019

    Steering and mats

    Added in-car steering wraps and laser-measured 7D mats, matched to the seat thread.

  4. 2021

    Avinashi Road studio

    Fitting bays, a customer lounge and the material room under one roof.

  5. 2026

    180+ models on file

    Full cabin makeovers and pick-up and drop across the city.
