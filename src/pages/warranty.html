What the warranty covers

Covered

  • {{icon check 18}}Loose or broken stitching (2 years PU and leatherette, 1 year suede and fabric)
  • {{icon check 18}}Zips, hooks and Velcro that fail in normal use
  • {{icon check 18}}Covers that loosen or sag: free re-tightening any time in warranty
  • {{icon check 18}}Airbag tear-away seams re-sewn after any airbag service
  • {{icon check 18}}Steering wrap seams coming apart

Not covered

  • {{icon close 18}}Cuts, burns or tears from sharp objects
  • {{icon close 18}}Damage from pets' claws or chemicals
  • {{icon close 18}}Fading from solvent cleaners
  • {{icon close 18}}Covers removed or refitted by someone else
  • {{icon close 18}}Normal wear on bolsters after 2 years

Care by material

Compare the finishes {{icon arrow 16}}

Nappa-grade PU

Weekly: damp microfibre cloth

Spills: blot, then wipe with pH-neutral cleaner

Avoid: alcohol, polish, heat guns

Perforated leatherette

Weekly: soft brush, then damp cloth

Spills: blot quickly so liquid stays out of holes

Avoid: soaking, spray directly on seat

Microsuede

Weekly: suede brush in one direction

Spills: dab with a barely damp cloth

Avoid: scrubbing, hair dryers

Jacquard fabric

Weekly: vacuum with upholstery head

Spills: mild soap water, dab and dry

Avoid: bleach, wringing
