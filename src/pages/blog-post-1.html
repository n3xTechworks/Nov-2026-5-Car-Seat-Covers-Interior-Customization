Leatherette, Nappa, fabric or mesh: choosing a seat cover material for Coimbatore

Heat, dust, kids and budget all pull in different directions. Here is how we help owners pick a material they will still like in year three.

Arjun RaghavanSenior pattern cutter · 12 Oct 2026 · 7 min read

Most people walk into the studio having already decided on 'leather'. Half of them leave with something else, and they are happier for it. The right material is the one that fits how you actually use the car: how long it is parked in the sun, who sits in the back, and how much time you want to spend on care.

Start with three questions

  1. Where is the car parked during the day? An open office lot in Peelamedu in April is a very different place from a covered basement.
  2. Who uses the rear seat? Children, pets and grocery runs need a wipe-clean surface.
  3. How long will you keep the car? A five-year ownership plan justifies a better material than a two-year lease.

Leatherette: the sensible default

Modern PU leatherette is not the sticky vinyl of the 1990s. It is soft, colour-fast and resists spills, which is why it is our most booked material. Ask for perforated centre panels: they let air move through the seat and take the edge off the afternoon heat.

Real leather: worth it if you will care for it

Genuine Nappa-grade leather ages beautifully, breathes better than PU and smells like a premium car should. It also needs conditioning every few months and does not forgive a pen left in a back pocket. If you are happy with a ten-minute care routine, it is the most rewarding choice.

Fabric and mesh: the cool options

Automotive fabric is the coolest to sit on and the easiest on the budget. Air-mesh goes further, with an open 3D weave that you can rinse out. Both suit owners who drive long highway stretches or park outdoors every day.

Studio tip

Bring the car in. Touching a swatch in your hand is not the same as seeing it on the seat in daylight. We hold samples against your seats before you commit.

Our short answer

  • Family car, parked outdoors: leatherette with perforated centres.
  • Luxury sedan, covered parking: Nappa-grade leather.
  • Highway commuter on a budget: automotive fabric.
  • Hot cabin, sweaty drives, gym bag on the seat: air-mesh.
