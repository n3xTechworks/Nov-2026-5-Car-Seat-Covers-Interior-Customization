FAQ

Questions before booking

Still unsure?

How long does fitting take?

3–4 hours for a 5-seater and up to 6 hours for a 7-seater. Full cabin makeovers take one to two days. Wait in the lounge or use free pick-up within city limits.

Will seat covers block my side airbags?

No. Every pattern for a car with seat-mounted airbags has a tear-away seam sewn with low-strength thread exactly over the factory seam, plus an airbag label. Read how airbag-safe covers work.

Do you have a pattern for my car?

We hold patterns for 180+ models sold in India since 2015. Pick your car in the ; if we don't have it yet, we make a template at your home or office for free.

Which material is best for Coimbatore weather?

For cars parked outdoors, perforated leatherette or breathable fabric stay cooler than solid leather. Nappa leather suits covered parking. Our material guide compares all four.

What does the warranty cover?

Two years on every stitch: seams, zips, elastic and fit. Cuts from sharp objects, burns and covers refitted by someone else are not covered. Details are on the warranty page.

Do I need to pay an advance?

No advance for seat covers: you pay after fitting. Full cabin makeovers need a 30% advance to order materials.

Can you fit at my home or office?

Template visits happen at your place. Fitting is done at the studio, where we have the tools and lighting to do it right, with free pick-up and drop within Coimbatore city limits.
