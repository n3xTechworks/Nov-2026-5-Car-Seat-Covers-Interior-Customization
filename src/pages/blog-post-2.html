Are seat covers safe with side airbags? What to check before you buy

Most modern cars hide an airbag inside the front seat bolster. A cover that is sewn shut over it can stop it from deploying. Here is how airbag-safe covers work.

Kavya IyerHead of fitting safety · 28 Sep 2026 · 6 min read

If your car has an 'AIRBAG' tag stitched on the outer side of the front seats, there is a side airbag inside the seat. In a side impact it bursts through a weak seam in the original upholstery in a few milliseconds. Put a thick cover over that seam and the airbag may deploy in the wrong direction or not at all.

How an airbag-safe cover works

A safe cover has a matching break-away seam in exactly the same place as the factory seam. We sew it with a special low-strength thread and leave the panel unlined, so the airbag tears through it as easily as it would the original.

  • The tear seam lines up with the factory seam within a few millimetres.
  • Low-tensile thread is used only on that seam, never on the rest of the cover.
  • An 'Airbag' label is sewn on the cover so the next person knows it is there.
  • No foam padding or quilting sits over the bolster on that side.

Universal covers are the risk

Stretch-fit universal covers are cut to fit dozens of cars, so they cannot know where your airbag seam is. Many are fully sewn and some add a layer of foam. If your car has seat-mounted airbags, avoid them.

What we check at the studio

  1. We confirm the airbag layout for your exact model, year and trim.
  2. The pattern on file already has the tear seam marked; new patterns get one before cutting.
  3. After fitting, the fitter checks the seam position on both front seats and signs the job card.

Studio tip

Not sure if your car has side airbags? Choose 'Not sure' in the quote form. We check the VIN and the seat tags at fitting and adjust the cover at no extra cost.

What about curtain and knee airbags?

Curtain airbags sit in the roof lining and knee airbags under the dashboard, so seat covers do not affect them. Steering wraps are designed to leave the airbag cover in the hub untouched.
