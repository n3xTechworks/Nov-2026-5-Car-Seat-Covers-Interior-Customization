Ambient lighting done right: a subtle glow, not a nightclub

A soft strip of light in the doors and footwells makes a cabin feel premium at night. Done badly, it reflects in the windscreen and drains the battery.

Vikram NairElectrical and lighting fitter · 16 Aug 2026 · 5 min read

Factory ambient lighting used to be reserved for luxury cars. Today a well-installed kit can give almost any car the same effect, as long as the light is indirect and the wiring is done properly.

Where to put the light

  • Along the dashboard trim line, facing down, never up at the glass.
  • Inside door pulls and door pockets.
  • Footwells, front and rear: the most useful spot at night.
  • Under the front seats for rear passengers.

Pick one colour and dim it

The best cabins we have done use a single warm colour at low brightness. Rainbow cycling modes look fun for a week and then become a distraction. Amber, warm white or ice blue suit most interiors.

Wiring matters

  1. Power the strips from an ignition-switched fuse using a proper add-a-fuse tap.
  2. Route cables behind trim panels, away from airbag modules and pedals.
  3. Use fibre-optic strips in the dashboard: they do not get hot.
  4. Link the brightness to the headlamp dimmer where the car allows it.

Studio tip

Avoid kits that plug into the 12 V socket and run cables across the floor. They come loose, they trip feet, and they stay on when the car is parked.

Will it affect my warranty?

A clean installation using fuse taps does not cut or splice factory wiring, and it can be removed without trace. We document every connection on your job card.
