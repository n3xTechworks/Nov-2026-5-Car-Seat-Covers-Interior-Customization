1. Quotes

Website estimates are guides. The studio confirms a fixed price within one working day; that confirmed price is valid for 14 days.

2. Bookings and slots

A fitting slot is held for 48 hours until you confirm on WhatsApp or by phone. Unconfirmed slots are released.

3. Payments

Seat covers are paid after fitting. Full cabin makeovers need a 30% advance to order materials; the rest is due on handover. We accept UPI, cards and bank transfer at the studio.

4. Fitting and pick-up

Free pick-up and drop within Coimbatore city limits. Please remove valuables. Our driver holds a valid licence and the car is insured while with us.

5. Changes and cancellations

Change materials free until cutting starts. After cutting, custom sets cannot be cancelled; advances for makeovers are refunded in full if you cancel before materials are ordered.

6. Warranty

See Warranty & care for what is covered. Warranty is void if covers are removed or refitted by someone else.

7. Liability

Our liability is limited to repair or replacement of the work we did. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
