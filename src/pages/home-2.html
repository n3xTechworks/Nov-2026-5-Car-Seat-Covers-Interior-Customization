Before you book

Questions about cabin makeovers

More answers on seat covers, airbags and fitting are on our contact page.

Can I do a makeover in stages?

Yes. Many owners start with Essential and add the steering wrap, lighting or roof liner later. We keep your material, quilt and thread choices on file so every later part matches.

Can I see the colours on my own car first?

Yes. Book a free 30-minute design session and bring the car. We try samples on your seats and show up to three combinations on a sample board before anything is cut.

Does ambient lighting cut into the car's wiring?

No. The fibre-optic strips run on their own fused harness powered from the ignition, so they switch off with the car. Fitting takes about 3 hours and the lighting carries a 1-year warranty.

How long will my car stay at the studio?

About 4 hours for Essential, one day for Signature and two days for Bespoke. Pick-up and drop are free within Coimbatore city limits.

What warranty does a makeover carry?

Each part keeps its own warranty: 2 years on PU and leatherette stitching, the steering wrap and 7D mats, and 1 year on suede, fabric and ambient lighting. Your warranty card lists every part.

Do SUVs and 7-seaters cost more?

Package prices are for a 5-seat hatchback or sedan. SUVs add ₹6,000 and 7-seaters add ₹11,000, and we confirm the exact price for your model in writing.
