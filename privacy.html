Privacy policy

What we collect when you ask for a quote, why, and how to have it deleted. Plain language first; the legal detail follows in each section.

On this page

  1. What we collect
  2. Why we use it
  3. WhatsApp and SMS
  4. Cookies
  5. Who we share with
  6. How long we keep it
  7. Your choices

1. What we collect

Your name, mobile number, email, car details (brand, model, year, registration if given), pick-up address and the choices you make in the quote form. We do not take payment details on this website.

2. Why we use it

To prepare your quote, hold a fitting slot, arrange pick-up and drop, honour your warranty and answer your messages. We do not sell your details.

3. WhatsApp and SMS

If you turn on WhatsApp updates we send quote, slot and ready-for-pickup messages only. Reply STOP at any time.

4. Cookies

Essential cookies remember your car selection and quote draft. Analytics cookies are optional and only load if you allow them in the cookie banner.

5. Who we share with

Our courier partner (for swatches), our messaging provider and our hosting provider, each only as needed to deliver the service. Each is bound by a written agreement to use your details for that purpose alone.

6. How long we keep it

Quote requests that do not go ahead are deleted after 12 months. Job records are kept for the warranty period plus 8 years for tax purposes.

7. Your choices

Ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time. Deleting job records ends warranty support for that job.

Questions or deletion requests: hello@hexstitch.in · we respond within 7 days.
